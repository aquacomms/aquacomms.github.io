SMS/Voice Notification Service — Terms & Conditions

AquaComms · Effective date: July 27, 2026

1. The program

AquaComms delivers utility service notifications — emergency alerts (such as boil water advisories), planned outage and repair notices, and service updates — by SMS and voice call, in English and Spanish, on behalf of participating water utilities.

2. Consent and enrollment

You are enrolled because you provided your phone number on your utility account or signup form and consented to receive service and emergency notifications. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any good or service.

3. Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with service conditions; typically a small number of messages per month, more during emergencies. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. Opt out / help

Reply STOP to cancel at any time; you will receive a single confirmation of your opt-out. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact your utility office.

5. Supplemental service

This service is supplemental to — not a replacement for — legally required notification methods used by your utility. Emergency information is provided on a best-effort basis; always follow official guidance from your utility and public safety agencies.

6. Privacy

Use of the service is subject to our Privacy Policy. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
