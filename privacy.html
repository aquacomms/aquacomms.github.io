Privacy Policy

AquaComms — SMS/voice notification service for utility customers.
Effective date: July 27, 2026

What this service is

AquaComms sends service and emergency notifications (for example: boil water advisories, planned outages, repair and maintenance notices, and service updates) by SMS text message and voice call to utility customers who have opted in by providing their phone number on their utility account or signup form.

Information we collect

We process the contact information provided by you or your utility: name, phone number, service address, and language preference, together with delivery records of the notifications sent to you.

Mobile information is never shared or sold

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Your phone number is used solely to deliver the utility notifications described above and is shared only with our telecommunications provider to the extent necessary to deliver messages.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies with utility service conditions — typically a small number of messages per month, with additional messages during emergencies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. Opt-outs are honored immediately and recorded. Reply HELP for help, or contact your utility office. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Data retention and security

Contact and delivery records are retained as required for utility compliance documentation and are protected with industry-standard safeguards. You may request access to or deletion of your contact information through your utility.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact your utility office, or the service operator via the address on the Terms & Conditions page.
